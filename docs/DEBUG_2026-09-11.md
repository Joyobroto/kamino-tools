## Confirmed bugs patched in this workspace

1. `execute.ts`: USD profit divided by mint decimals twice. `usdPerBaseUnit` already divides the oracle token price by `10^decimals`; profit conversion divided again. A $1 USDC profit became $0.000001. Both simulated and blind branches now multiply base units by the Decimal base-unit price directly, retaining precision until conversion to number.
2. `estimateCollateralForRepay`: protocol fee applied to principal plus bonus. At 1% bonus and 50% protocol fee the old model returned 50.399% of principal after the 20 bps haircut. The corrected estimate returns 100.299%. Underestimating swap input can leave too little debt token to repay the flash loan or produce a negative profit guard.

Fee semantics checked against the official KLend source: https://github.com/Kamino-Finance/klend/blob/master/programs/klend/src/lending_market/lending_operations.rs (post_liquidate_redeem).

Regression coverage: USDC/SOL base-unit conversions, negative profit, $0.05 boundary, protocol share of bonus, zero-bonus principal preservation. Build and existing test suite pass. These tests validate arithmetic, not a successful mainnet liquidation. The collateral helper remains an estimate with a haircut, not a bit-exact reproduction of all on-chain rounding and liquidation branches.

## Structural issues addressed since the initial audit

The route wait, queue reservation, race attribution, stale watchlist claims, missing failure telemetry, compute-budget composition, and automatic simulation bypass called out below have been patched. The watchboard now shows estimated margin after protocol/flash fees and explicitly excludes swap/network costs. A synthetic benchmark is recorded in `docs/APP_SPEED_BENCHMARK.json`.

## Structural issues still present

- **Quote latency:** background fallback quote requests are bounded by deadlines but are not cancellable once started. This can still consume provider/server resources during a burst; it no longer blocks the first usable route.
- **Race attribution:** the normal veto path now uses a bounded public-RPC history window and exact instruction matching. Historical ledger rows remain unverifiable because they predate the extra fields.
- **Tracker labels:** `taken` remains a compatibility event for a tracker exit. Console and Telegram wording now says the liquidation is unverified until forensics finds a successful transaction.
- **Concurrency:** executor capacity is reserved synchronously and covered by a burst test.
- **Observability:** new rows retain rail, trigger time/slot, total latency, stage timings, simulation status, and bounded simulation logs. Older rows cannot be retroactively repaired.
- **Economics beyond this patch:** the profit guard subtracts flash fee but not SOL transaction fees/priority fee/ATA rent. A positive quoted token surplus is not necessarily positive final USD P&L. At 1% gross bonus, 50% protocol share and 20 bps haircut, only roughly 30 bps remains before swap slippage and flash/network fees; a 50 bps slippage floor can legitimately reject the trade even after correcting arithmetic.
- **Automatic execution:** the small-prize simulation bypass was removed. The low-level executor option still exists for deliberate callers, but the daemon is simulation-gated.
- **Coverage/sizing:** non-vanilla obligations are explicitly excluded. Repay sizing uses 10% of the selected borrow rather than fully modeling total-debt caps, full liquidation of small debts and available collateral. Official current source contains additional cases; validate against deployed program/market before expanding support.

## Suggested implementation order

Review the arithmetic patch, retain a simulation-based validation path, then fix attribution/telemetry and quote waiting/concurrency. Replay or simulate representative profitable and dust cases before rebuilding the live image. Infrastructure spending should follow measured trigger-to-hydrate-to-send timings; the present evidence does not isolate RPC geography or priority bid as the primary latest failure.
